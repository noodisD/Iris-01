# errors.new_kinds

**What it means:** A kind of error appeared in the last day that the week before had not seen.

**Confirm:** Observatory → errors, filtered by the span names in `details.new`.

**Safe fix:** Find the change or provider behaviour behind it; known transient kinds stop being new after a day.

**Never:** Never paste exception messages into issues or chat: they can quote a source.

See [README](README.md) for action classes and the rules every check follows.
